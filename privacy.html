Privacy Policy — Venture Station

Effective date: 2026-09-24

Venture Station is a small, owner-operated online business tool. This policy explains how it uses Google account data.

What we access

Venture Station accesses only the owner's own Gmail account (dogdoozystore@gmail.com), using the Gmail "gmail.modify" scope. No other person's Gmail account, and no other Google account, is accessed.

Why we access it

Access is used to read, send, and organize the business's own email — for example, retrieving sign-up or verification codes needed to operate business accounts, and reading or sending customer and campaign correspondence on the owner's behalf.

How data is used and shared

Gmail data is not sold. It is not shared with third parties, except with the AI model providers used to process it on the owner's behalf (for example, to help read, draft, or organize messages). It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train generalized AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Venture Station's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You (the account owner) can revoke Venture Station's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: dogdoozystore@gmail.com
